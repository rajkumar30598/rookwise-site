Questions?

Is Rookwise free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions and no limits. Every feature is included.

When can I get it?

Rookwise is in testing on Google Play. Tap "Join the early test" and you'll get an invite before the public launch.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Lessons, puzzles, review, the bot and analysis all run on your phone. Importing games and live broadcasts need a connection.

Does it work with Lichess and Chess.com?

Sign in to Lichess or link a Chess.com username, and your finished games are imported for review and puzzles.

Can it help me during an online game?

No, on purpose. Rookwise is for learning and reviewing finished games.
