The app

From your first move to your best game.

Five things Rookwise does for you, all on your phone and all free.

01 — Learn

Start from zero.

44 short lessons take you from how the pieces move to forks, famous mates and simple endgames. Practise on the board, earn stars, and climb from Pawn to King.

  • Read aloud in your language
  • Practice rounds
  • Middlegame & endgame units
02 — Review

A coach for every move.

Import your Lichess and Chess.com games. Every move is rated from Brilliant to Blunder, with accuracy, key moments and the move that punishes each mistake.

  • Game report
  • "Why is this bad?"
  • Retry your mistakes
03 — Train

Your mistakes, now puzzles.

Every blunder in your games becomes a puzzle, repeated until you've mastered it. Plus a daily puzzle, Puzzle Rush, an opening trainer built from your own lines and classic endgames.

  • Daily streak & widget
  • Opening explorer
  • 12 famous traps
04 — Play

A bot that teaches back.

From beginner to around 3200, in four styles. Live hints, an evaluation bar and a coach that rates your moves while you play. Blunder check stops you before the bot punishes a slip.

  • Chess960 & odds
  • Blindfold & voice
  • Pass and play
05 — Watch

Bot vs Bot, explained.

Two Rookwise Bots, each with its own strength and style, play at your pace. Every move rated, the better one shown. Guess the best move before they play it.

  • 0.5 to 10 seconds a move
  • Pause & step through
  • Live broadcasts too

Questions

Good to know.

Is Rookwise free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no limits. Every feature is included.

When can I get it?

Rookwise is in testing on Google Play. Tap "Join the early test" and send the email, and you'll get an invite before the public release.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Lessons, puzzles, game review, the bot and analysis all run on your phone. Importing games and live broadcasts need a connection.

Does it work with Lichess and Chess.com?

Sign in to Lichess or link a Chess.com username, and your finished games are imported for review and turned into puzzles.

Can it help me during an online game?

No, on purpose. Rookwise is for learning and reviewing finished games, and it never asks Lichess for access to games in progress.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Rookwise launches on Android first.
