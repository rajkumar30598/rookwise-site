Questions

Good to know.

Is Rookwise free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions and no limits. Every feature is included.

When can I get it?

Rookwise is in testing on Google Play. Send a quick email with "Join the early test" and you'll get an invite to install it before the public release.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Lessons, puzzles, game review, the bot and analysis all run on your phone. Importing games and live broadcasts need a connection.

Can I use it with my Lichess or Chess.com account?

Yes. Sign in to Lichess or link a Chess.com username, and your finished games are imported for review and turned into puzzles.

Can it help me during an online game?

No, and that's on purpose. Rookwise is for learning and reviewing finished games, and it never asks Lichess for access to games in progress.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Rookwise launches on Android first.
